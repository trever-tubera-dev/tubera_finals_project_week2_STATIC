# STATIC — HCI Project Proposal & Handoff

## Project Overview

**STATIC** is an alternative music community and customizable profile website inspired by early MySpace, personal profile websites, and underground internet culture. It gives users a place to share their music taste, customize their own profiles, and discover other people with similar interests.

## User Problem

Most mainstream music platforms mainly focus on listening to music and getting recommendations. While they are useful for discovering songs and artists, they don't really give users much freedom to express their music taste or customize a page based on their personality and interests. STATIC aims to provide a space where people can show what they listen to, express their style, and connect with others who enjoy similar music and subcultures.

## Target Audience

The website is mainly for people who listen to alternative music or are interested in related subcultures. This includes fans of Midwest emo, screamo/skramz, shoegaze, math rock, indie rock, alternative rock, nu metal, metalcore, heavy metal, bedroom grunge, emo, punk, and scene culture.

## Chosen Aesthetic Theme

The website will have a dark and underground look inspired by early-2000s internet designs, alternative music zines, and grunge layouts. The main interface will use dark backgrounds, muted text, and a few accent colors. However, user profiles should be customizable instead of all looking the same. Users should be able to use their own colors, graphics, stickers, patterns, and other decorations, especially for those who prefer a more colorful scene-inspired style.

## Planned Interface Goals

- Allow users to create and customize their own profiles.
- Give users a place to display their favorite artists, albums, songs, and playlists.
- Let users show what they are currently listening to or add supported music embeds.
- Help users find new artists, explore genres, and discover other people.
- Provide a Scene Wall where users can share photos, outfits, artwork, and other posts.
- Include a guestbook or messaging feature where users can interact with each other.

## Planned Main Features

- **Personal Profiles:** Users can customize their pages with profile details, backgrounds, colors, and decorations.
- **Music Collection:** A section for displaying favorite artists, albums, songs, and playlists.
- **Currently Listening:** A place to show what a user is currently listening to or embed music from supported platforms.
- **Discover:** Lets users explore different artists, genres, albums, and other profiles.
- **Scene Wall:** A space for sharing photos, outfits, artwork, and other things related to alternative music and its subcultures.
- **Guestbook:** Allows visitors to leave messages on other users' profiles.

## Week 1 and Week 2 Checkpoint Scope

For Week 1, the focus is on setting up the project folder, preparing the proposal, and defining the initial design through global CSS variables.

For Week 2, the project will have its basic website structure using semantic HTML elements, the four required navigation links (HOME, ABOUT, GALLERY, CONTACT), a main interface card, and a contact form with Full Name, Email Address, and Message fields. It will also include local image references and use Flexbox to center the main content on the page.

The contact form is only for the website's layout at this stage. It will not actually send or save messages since there is no backend or form service connected yet.
