About STATIC

STATIC is a place to find underground music and show who you are while you listen. Browse releases by genre, then visit a profile to see what someone is playing and leave them a message.

Built as an HCI class project. Inspired by early-2000s internet culture, DIY music zines, and small independent record labels.
